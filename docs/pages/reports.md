# Reports

**Sidebar entry:** Reports
**Source:** `src/features/reports/reports-page.tsx`

The analytics screen: revenue trends, plot-status and reservation-status
breakdowns, and a log of generated reports. All charts are hand-rolled SVG
components (`BarChart`, `AreaChart`, `DonutChart`) — no charting library.

## Page structure

### KPI strip

Total Clients, Reservations, Payments, and Agreements (whole dataset).

### Filters card

- **Month** — All Months, or any `YYYY-MM` present in the payment data
  (derived from `payments.date`).
- **Cemetery** — All Cemeteries, or one cemetery.
- **Generate** — applies the chosen month/cemetery to every chart below.
- **Clear Filters** — resets to all/all.

The selects are staged locally; nothing changes until **Generate** is pressed.

### Revenue charts

- **Monthly Revenue** — area chart of Paid payments grouped by calendar month,
  with the total and the month-over-month change (▲/▼ %).
- **Weekly Revenue** — bar chart of Paid payments grouped by week (weeks start
  on Sunday), same total/change header.

Only payments with status `Paid` count toward revenue. Both charts respect the
applied cemetery/month filter.

### Breakdowns

- **Plot Status Breakdown** — donut of Available/Reserved/Occupied/Waiting with
  the occupancy rate (Occupied + Reserved ÷ total) in the center.
- **Reservations by Status** — bar chart of Pending / Under Verification /
  Confirmed / Cancelled counts.

### Recent Reports table

A paginated (8/page) log of generated reports: name, type, date generated,
generated by, status badge (Ready / Processing), and Edit/Delete actions.

## Data

Everything is computed client-side from the shared staff store (payments,
plots, reservations, clients, agreements, and the `generated_reports` table).
